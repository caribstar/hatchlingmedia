The Fix-It Twins

Bible & Manuscript Complete

Ten-year-old twins discover that fixing things is really about fixing what matters — family, friendship, and community.

Concept Summary

Emma and Jack Henderson spend afternoons in Grandpa Joe's workshop, learning repair skills that translate into life lessons. Set in Maplewood, USA. Champions STEM education, repair culture, sustainability, and intergenerational bonding. Book 1 manuscript complete at ~11,000 words with 10-episode season outline and STEM curriculum guide.

Recipe Rescue Crew

Concept & Design

A group of kid cooks race to save family recipes before they're lost forever — learning kitchen science and celebrating cultural heritage along the way.

Concept Summary

Four diverse kids discover a problem-solving cooking format that blends kitchen science with multicultural family heritage. Each episode rescues a recipe from a different cultural tradition while teaching real nutrition and food science. Fills a gap for cooking content where kids are problem-solvers, not performers.

Target Audience & Platform

Seed Savers Society

Concept & Design

A secret club of young gardeners uncovers mysteries hidden in their town's oldest seeds — and learns why biodiversity matters before it's too late.

Concept Summary

Five kids discover a hidden seed vault beneath their community garden and form a secret society to protect endangered plant species. Part environmental mystery, part adventure series. Makes biodiversity tangible through kid-driven storytelling without being preachy. Real science woven into compelling narrative arcs.
