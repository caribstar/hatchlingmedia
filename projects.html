🏃 Speedeet & Wilar

Caribbean adventures with heart and humor

About the Series

Follow best friends Speedeet and Wilar, two 12-year-old boys from Pike Street in Kitty, Georgetown, Guyana, as they navigate childhood adventures with wit, mischief, and genuine friendship. Written in authentic Guyanese Creolese, these stories celebrate Caribbean culture and the universal experience of growing up.

The series includes both hometown adventures in Guyana and an expanding "Caribbean Adventures" collection featuring Jamaica, Trinidad, and more.

🔧 The Fix-It Twins

"If it's broken, we're on it."

About the Series

When their grandfather hurts his back, 10-year-old twins Emma and Jack Henderson must take over his beloved neighborhood repair shed. With Grandpa Joe guiding them from his recliner, the twins discover that fixing broken toasters, radios, and music boxes teaches them something bigger: how to fix problems, relationships, and themselves.

A heartwarming STEM-focused series that celebrates repair culture, intergenerational wisdom, and the power of learning through failure.

Main Characters

Emma Henderson (The Doer) Jack Henderson (The Thinker) Grandpa Joe (The Mentor)

Key Themes

Problem-solving • Learning from failure • Intergenerational bonds • Community • Real skills over magic solutions

🍳 The Recipe Rescue Crew

"When the recipe goes wrong, we make it right!"

About the Series

When Nana Rosa breaks her arm right before the town's annual food festival, 9-year-old cousins Mia (the rusher) and Theo (the measurer) must recreate her legendary dishes from cryptic recipe cards—with reluctant help from Uncle Ray, who hasn't cooked since he burned ramen in college.

Every dish is a disaster before it's a success. Every failure teaches kitchen science. And every recipe reveals a story about where their multicultural family came from.

Main Characters

Mia Santos (The Rusher) Theo Santos (The Measurer) Uncle Ray (The Reluctant Adult) Nana Rosa (The Oracle)

Key Themes

Kitchen science • Family heritage • Multicultural traditions • Learning from failure • Food brings people together

🌱 The Seed Savers Society

"Every seed has a story. Every garden has a secret."

About the Series

Eleven-year-old Maya discovers her late grandfather's hidden seed collection and a mysterious journal revealing he was part of a secret network of "Seed Savers" who protect rare and heirloom plants from extinction. Now it's her turn to carry on the mission with her friends.

Part mystery, part gardening guide, this series teaches real seed-saving skills while following Maya and her friends as they race to save endangered plant varieties before they're lost forever.

Main Characters

Maya Johnson (The Inheritor) Omar Ahmed (The Researcher) Birdie Nguyen (The Connector) Miss Patrice (The Network Node)

Key Themes

Biodiversity • Real gardening skills • Mystery & adventure • Cultural heritage through plants • Community action
