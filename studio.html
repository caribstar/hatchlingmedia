The Studio & Creator

An independent creative studio building original worlds for global audiences.

Albert Massay

Founder & Creator

Albert Massay is a storyteller and creator developing original animated worlds and characters with long-term franchise potential. His work focuses on creating authentic, culturally rich stories while building scalable properties suitable for digital platforms, streaming services, and brand partnerships.

A Guyanese-American creator, Massay brings a deep commitment to Caribbean cultural preservation into his storytelling. His published series, Speedeet & Wilar, is written entirely in authentic Guyanese Creolese — the first children's book series of its kind — and celebrates the humor, warmth, and spirit of Caribbean life for a global audience.

Through Hatchling Media, Massay is building a portfolio of original intellectual property spanning animation, publishing, and educational content, with each project designed from the ground up for multi-platform growth and partnership readiness.

About the Studio

Hatchling Media is an independent creative studio founded by creator Albert Massay, focused on developing original intellectual property for animation, digital entertainment, and licensing opportunities.

The studio specializes in building character-driven stories rooted in humor, community, and relatable experiences, designed to resonate with global family and youth audiences. Every project begins with original characters and worlds, developed through a repeatable process from concept to production-ready packaging.

Studio Philosophy

We believe strong characters and relatable worlds are the foundation of enduring entertainment franchises. Our goal is to develop stories that entertain audiences while creating opportunities for long-term brand and licensing growth.

Creator-Led Development

Every project originates with a singular creative vision, ensuring authentic storytelling and consistent world-building from concept through production.

Partnership-Ready Packaging

We develop series bibles, character designs, scripts, and production materials that enable studios and distributors to move quickly.

Scalable IP Architecture

Each property is designed for multi-platform growth — from books and animation to licensing, merchandise, and educational programs.

Cultural Authenticity

We create stories rooted in real communities and genuine voices, bringing underrepresented cultures and perspectives to global audiences.
