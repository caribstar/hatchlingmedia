The Studio & Creator

An independent creative studio building original worlds for global audiences.

Albert Massay

Founder & Creator

Albert Massay is a creator and storyteller focused on building original character-driven worlds for children and family audiences. He is the founder of Hatchling Media, an independent studio dedicated to developing IP for animation, publishing, and global media partnerships.

In 2014, Massay was injured while serving in Afghanistan. During the long convalescence that followed, he began writing what would become the Speedeet & Wilar stories — a comedy adventure series set in a vibrant Caribbean neighborhood that transforms ordinary childhood moments into imaginative adventures. Over the following years, those early stories grew into a series of published books and a fully realized world centered on friendship and neighborhood adventure.

His work draws on authentic Caribbean childhood experiences, blending humor, imagination, and everyday adventure into stories that resonate worldwide. Massay’s projects emphasize friendship, creativity, and cultural authenticity while remaining universally accessible.

After years of development, Massay set a 2026 resolution to take Speedeet & Wilar into full animated production while expanding Hatchling Media’s slate with additional original properties for licensing and partnership — establishing the foundation for a long-term creator-led studio.

Through Hatchling Media, Massay partners with studios, distributors, and brands to bring culturally rich, globally appealing stories to life — building a studio that continuously creates original worlds and characters while expanding representation in children’s media.

About the Studio

Hatchling Media is an independent creative studio focused on developing original intellectual property for animation, digital entertainment, and licensing opportunities.

The studio specializes in building character-driven stories rooted in humor, community, and relatable childhood experiences, designed to resonate with global youth and family audiences.

Studio Philosophy

We believe strong characters and relatable worlds form the foundation of enduring entertainment franchises. Hatchling Media develops stories that entertain while supporting long-term growth through partnerships and licensing opportunities.
