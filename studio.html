The Studio & Creator

An independent creative studio building original intellectual property for animation, digital entertainment, and licensing.

Hatchling Media is an independent creative studio founded by creator Albert Massay, focused on developing original intellectual property for animation, digital entertainment, and licensing opportunities.

The studio specializes in building character-driven stories rooted in humor, community, and relatable childhood experiences, designed to resonate with global youth and family audiences.

Albert Massay

Founder & Creator

Albert Massay is a storyteller and creator focused on building original animated worlds and characters with long-term franchise potential. His work combines authentic cultural storytelling with scalable development designed for digital platforms, streaming services, and licensing expansion.

With published works across multiple book series and an active pipeline of IP in development, Albert brings a creator-first approach to intellectual property development — building stories that entertain while creating assets designed for partnership growth.

Studio Philosophy

We believe strong characters and relatable worlds form the foundation of enduring entertainment franchises. Hatchling Media develops stories that entertain while supporting long-term growth through partnerships and licensing opportunities.
