Speedeet & Wilar

Available for Licensing

Two twelve-year-old best friends from Pike Street, Kitty, Georgetown tackle everyday fun and cultural adventures across the Caribbean with heart, humor, and resilience.

Project Overview

Speedeet (Black) and Wilar (East Indian) are inseparable friends navigating childhood in Guyana and beyond. The series blends authentic Creolese dialogue with universal themes of friendship, cultural identity, and growing up. Published across two series — Pike Street neighborhood adventures and Caribbean Adventures spanning Jamaica, Trinidad, and Guyana — with supporting characters including Ma, Aunty Devi, Lil Suzie, Old Man Doodnauth, and Miss Persaud.

The Fix-It Twins

Pilot Ready

Ten-year-old twins Emma and Jack Henderson discover that fixing things is really about fixing what matters — family, friendship, and community.

Project Overview

Set in the suburban neighborhood of Maplewood, USA, The Fix-It Twins follows Emma (the bold, action-oriented doer) and Jack (the cautious, analytical thinker) as they learn repair skills from Grandpa Joe in his backyard shed. Each episode weaves STEM education, problem-solving, and community building into warm, family-centered storytelling. The series is fully compliant with YouTube Kids and Amazon Kids content guidelines.

Recipe Rescue Crew

In Development

A group of kid cooks race to save family recipes before they're lost forever — learning kitchen science along the way.

Recipe Rescue Crew combines cooking, cultural heritage preservation, and kitchen science into an adventure series where young protagonists discover that every recipe holds a story.
