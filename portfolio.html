Speedeet & Wilar

Available for Licensing

Two best friends tackle everyday fun and cultural adventures with heart, humor, and resilience — celebrating Caribbean life through authentic Guyanese Creolese storytelling.

Speedeet
Wilar

Speedeet & Wilar is a culturally rich, humor-driven children's IP centered on two twelve-year-old friends from Pike Street, Kitty, Georgetown, Guyana. Written entirely in authentic Guyanese Creolese, the series is the first of its kind — bringing Caribbean youth culture and language to a global audience.

The franchise spans two published book series and a complete 26-episode animation-ready season, with supporting characters, a full series bible, and HeyGen video prototypes already produced.

The Fix-It Twins

In Development

Ten-year-old twins discover that fixing things is really about fixing what matters — family, friendship, and community.

The Fix-It Twins follows Emma and Jack Henderson, ten-year-old siblings who spend their afternoons in Grandpa Joe's workshop learning to repair broken objects — and in the process, learning to repair what really counts. Set in the fictional town of Maplewood, the series champions repair culture, STEM learning, and intergenerational connection.

With a complete Book 1 manuscript, 10-episode season outline, full character profiles, and STEM curriculum guide, this IP is packaged for animation, publishing, and educational licensing.

In Development

Each episode blends cooking adventure with cultural storytelling and kitchen science, creating a format that entertains while teaching nutrition, food science, and the importance of family heritage.

Seed Savers Society

In Development

A secret club of young gardeners uncovers mysteries hidden in their town's oldest seeds — and learns why biodiversity matters before it's too late.

Seed Savers Society follows five kids who discover a hidden seed vault beneath their community garden and form a secret society to protect rare plant species from disappearing. Part environmental mystery, part adventure series, this IP makes biodiversity tangible and urgent through kid-driven storytelling.
